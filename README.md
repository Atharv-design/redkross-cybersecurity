## 🎯 What is this Website?

This website is the dedicated home for anyone interested in the technical, computing, and systems research initiatives conducted by RedKross.

The Cyber Wing was established to take computer science and software concepts out of purely theoretical textbooks and place them into the hands of students and young researchers. Through real-world software projects, hands-on simulation environments, and college outreach workshops, we help the next generation of engineers understand how modern digital systems work, how they behave under pressure, and how to build resilient technology.

---

## 🔍 What Can You Explore on this Website?

### 1. Six Core Technology Focus Areas
Interactive deep-dives into key computing domains:
- **Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning:** Understanding model behaviors, evaluation frameworks, and testing intelligent systems.
- **Defensive Systems & Software Architecture:** Exploring resilient code design, operating system internals, and observability.
- **Digital Forensics & Data Investigation:** Techniques for analyzing digital footprints, memory traces, and system artifacts.
- **Telemetry & Threat Analysis:** Tracking digital attack patterns and understanding large-scale network telemetry.
- **Cloud & IoT Computing:** Exploring microservices, containerized setups, and edge device networks.
- **Human-Centred Technology:** Digital literacy, usability of tools, and building intuitive technology that anyone can navigate safely.

### 2. Practical Projects Directory
A catalog of open-source tools, educational utilities, and software prototypes developed by fellows and student researchers. Each project entry provides:
- The real-world problem it solves
- How the system is designed and structured
- Live code links and operational documentation

### 3. Simulation Labs & Testbeds
An inside look at our virtual lab environments where students and fellows can safely test software configurations, simulate enterprise network setups, and conduct stress testing without affecting production systems.

### 4. College Partnerships & Campus Workshops
A structured 5-stage workshop program designed for engineering colleges and universities:
- Hands-on technical sessions led by experienced fellows
- Practical labs in software troubleshooting, system analysis, and cloud tools
- Clear guidance for institutions to schedule campus training

### 5. Research Fellowships & Student Internships
Information on how students and aspiring developers can apply to join active research cohorts, collaborate with experienced mentors, and contribute to meaningful technology projects.

### 6. Learning Resources & Guides
Curated open-access guides, checklists, and references for students, self-learners, and tech enthusiasts looking to build their practical skills.
